Desk Workers

How It Helps

Stretching alone doesn't fix the way you sit

Relief that fades by evening means the mechanics were never addressed. Spine Care works on the mechanics themselves — in order:

01

Posture, corrected at the source

Proper sitting and screen mechanics, and the office ergonomics to hold them — so the day stops working against your spine.

02

Alignment, rebuilt

Targeted postures realign the spine and release the tension bad posture locks in place — the same work extends to shoulder and knee patterns.

03

Strength, to hold it

Spine-specific strengthening keeps the correction in place after the class ends — the difference between relief and a habit.

A separate, age-appropriate Spine Care program is taught for children — ask us for details.
