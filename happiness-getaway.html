Frequently Asked Questions

What are the dates and timings of the Happiness Program?

The three-day program takes place from 27–29 November 2026. Friday’s session is from 4:00–7:00 PM, and Saturday and Sunday’s sessions are from 10:00 AM–1:00 PM.

Where is the course being held?

The venue is Gurukul Veda Life – Goa, House No. 37, Patnem, Canacona, South Goa, Goa – 403702. View on Google Maps.

How do I register for the Happiness Program?

Register through the official link: aolt.in/1067829. Course registration and accommodation arrangements are separate.

How much is the Happiness Program?

The contribution is ₹1,000 per person for the three-day program, taught in English. Accommodation is separate.

Do I need to book accommodation through the organizers?

No. You can arrange your own accommodation and register only for the course. Alternatively, get in touch to enquire about staying at the course venue.

How much does the shared accommodation cost?

Accommodation costs ₹6,000 per person for one bed in a two-person shared room. This is separate from course registration.

Is food included in the accommodation?

No. Food is not included. The accommodation has a kitchen where participants can prepare their own meals.

Can I attend the Happiness Program without staying at the venue?

Yes. You can register for the course and arrange your own accommodation elsewhere.

Do I need to come with a group?

Not at all. Whether you’re travelling with friends, coming as a couple, or joining on your own, the experience is designed to work for you. Connect with others, enjoy shared experiences, and still have plenty of time to explore Goa — or simply have some time to yourself.

What does the experience include?

The experience combines the Happiness Program and Craniosacral Therapy with time to relax, connect and enjoy Goa.

What is the Happiness Program?

A guided experience from Art of Living that combines practical wisdom, breathing practices, meditation and interactive processes to help you manage stress, reconnect with yourself and bring greater ease and balance into everyday life.

What is Craniosacral Therapy (CST)?

A gentle, hands-on wellness therapy that works with subtle rhythms and the body’s natural patterns. It is designed as a deeply relaxing experience that encourages rest, release and a sense of wellbeing.

Is this a spiritual retreat?

It includes breathing, meditation and the Happiness Program, but the overall experience is designed to be relaxed, grounded and vacation-oriented.
